Painted tables

Blackjack, baccarat and craps layouts, painted on the felt: every word, line, box, die and scroll is a paintFelt mark in one of the table paints - white, cream, yellow, gold, red, black, blue, green - or any colour, and an ordinary sprite under the cards. The parlour table is old patterns drawn in code: guilloché, the Greek key and seigaiha waves. Drag the cards about over it.
