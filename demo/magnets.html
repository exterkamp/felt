Magnetic stacks

Four stacks, one for each suit. Pick up the top card and flick it towards its suit: near enough, the stack draws it in and squares it up - curving it in as it slides by, or pulling it the last of the way if it stops close. Other suits' stacks don't pull it. Too fast and it gets away; a card left loose on the felt can be flicked again.

Flick each card at its suit.
