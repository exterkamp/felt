Readouts & toasts

The score, the clock and the pile labels are readouts: glyphs on the board, painted once and reused, so bumping the score paints nothing. Banners and floats are toastsOf(board), a layer pinned over the canvas. Everything said to a screen reader goes through announce — and is logged below. Tap the stock to turn a card; tap the waste to send it home.
